# Thigh IMU Mounting System

**Status: work in progress**

This subsystem develops an adjustable strap-mounted IMU interface for the thigh cuff of the hip-knee exoskeleton. The mechanism is intended to let the sensor attachment move up or down and side-to-side within the cuff opening so the IMU can be fitted at a more comparable relative position across participants.

## Mechanical concept

1. A strap-routing attachment is captured within the opening in the thigh cuff.
2. The attachment can translate vertically and side-to-side within that opening for participant-specific positioning.
3. The retention strap threads through the movable attachment and then wraps around the thigh and cuff.
4. Bolts installed through the opposite side of the thigh cuff bound the attachment's vertical travel, reducing the risk that it will slip beyond the intended adjustment region.

This adjustable strap path is meant to combine repeatable IMU placement with a practical wearable attachment. More consistent placement should reduce setup-dependent variation in gait data and may improve transfer of control or estimation algorithms between participants. That data and algorithm benefit remains a design hypothesis requiring experimental validation.

## Current development questions

- Is the available up/down and side-to-side adjustment sufficient for the intended participant population?
- Do the cuff bolts reliably bound vertical travel without interfering with fitting or strap tension?
- Does the tightened strap prevent the attachment and IMU from shifting during gait?
- Can the same fitting procedure reproduce relative sensor position and initial orientation across participants?
